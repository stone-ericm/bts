# Verified 2026-10-04 ~01:20 ET: BTS 2026 Official Rules, prize terms (curl with a browser UA; https://www.mlb.com/apps/beat-the-streak/official-rules; page 906,050 bytes)

- **§7:** one Grand Prize ($5.6M; present value $3M as of 3/12/2026) OR one Top Streak Prize ($10,000). "No prize will be awarded if no entrant achieves a Streak of at least twenty (20)."
- **§8(b), verbatim:** "if no entrant wins the Grand Prize, the eligible entrant (if any) who, as of the close of the BTS Entry Period, has achieved the highest Streak among all entries and provided such Streak twenty (20) or more, will win the Top Streak Prize. In the event of a tie (...the highest scoring Streak of twenty (20) or more is achieved by multiple players), the Grand Prize or Top Streak Prize, as applicable, will be divided equally among the tied, eligible participants."
- **§8:** "The winner of the Top Streak Prize does not have to have an active Streak at the close of the BTS Entry Period."

**Implication for D1 (reasoning only):** the late-season objective that pays is relative: P(our season best ≥ the field's best and ≥ 20). It is not E[our season best], the tail policy's objective. Our 2026 best was 18, below the 20 floor.
