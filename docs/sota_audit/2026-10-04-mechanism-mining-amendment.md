## A2. Consensus slots, ties and legal pairs

Count distinct cohort users by `batter_id` for each date and slot; names are display fields and do not split votes. Slot 1 is the highest-count primary id. Slot 2 is the highest-count double-down id after excluding the chosen slot-1 id. Apply this rule separately to the fixed and all-tracked cohorts. Consensus share uses the original number of users with a valid id in that slot; excluding a candidate does not renormalize the denominator. Select ids before joining or interpreting outcomes.

Break a tie at the eligible maximum count by lowest `batter_id`. Set `consensus_tie_broken` for a directly tied choice and for a DD choice dependent on a tied primary; record direct and dependent reasons separately. Ties exposed by excluding the primary count as ties. Record whether the legal DD differs from the unconstrained mode.

If primary consensus is unavailable or no distinct legal DD id exists, preserve an existing production DD unit with legal consensus unavailable. Do not duplicate the primary, drop the production unit or choose a replacement based on outcome availability. A date without production DD has no slot-2 unit. Conflicting settlement evidence remains unresolved unless the frozen resolver establishes the relevant settlement; voting popularity is not an outcome resolver.

The primary includes all retained units, with each estimand's prescribed resolution/provenance denominator. The sole sensitivity removes flagged units from the already fixed selections, separately within each cohort, without choosing another winner. Report primary candidates that do not pass the sensitivity, distinguishing effect, q-value and support losses. The sensitivity cannot nominate a candidate that fails the primary.
