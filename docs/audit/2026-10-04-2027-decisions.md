## D1. What should the system optimize next season?

**The situation.** Our contest best was 18. Under the reviewed 2026 rules, the $10,000 Top Streak Prize requires the highest eligible streak of at least 20, splits eligible ties, and is payable only if no Grand Prize is awarded. Winning that prize, expected prize share and our own expected season best are different objectives. We have not estimated the joint field/own distribution needed for either prize objective. Current 2027 rules must be checked before activation.

**The choice.**
- **Option 1:** prioritize the projected chance of 57, using season best only to break ties.
- **Option 2:** accept a specified reduction in projected jackpot chance for a better expected longest streak.

**Evidence.** The valid census of 121,852 entrants retained a maximum season-best streak of 39, attained by one entrant; 2,186 reached at least 20, 49 at least 30, and none 40. Our 18 lay in the 96.2–97.5 percentile tie interval, not a statistical confidence interval. The census is the September 27 board, with an identical September 28 post-cutoff pass cited. It does not establish the leader's eligibility, official closing standings or an awarded prize.

The following is historical realized-sequence replay on estimated-PA profiles for 2021–2025, without a leg-probability haircut. The season-best column records only what the frozen July memo supplies; it is not a complete 2027 expected-value frontier.

The 120 trajectories reuse five seasons across 24 seeds; they are not 120 independent seasons. Even estimated-PA profiles condition on realized participation. The replay is comparator parity, not exact production parity: its 180-position clock drops 312 of 21,888 paired rows, and a partnerless double advances by two where production demotes to a single (0.46% of days; the source's correction moved mean-max by less than 0.001). New policy acceptance must fix or disclose these shortcuts and replay legal actions through the original calendar.

The lower-milestone results show policy-dependent iid error, not a general rule that doubling improves every objective. In the source's 0.139 leg-shortfall stress cell, always-single mean-max is 0.79 above deployed even though its reach-20 rate remains lower. These are scenarios, not an estimate of the true leg shortfall. The September tail audit validated application of the season-best objective, not its value or optimality.

**Reading it (reasoning, not a measurement).** Eric can state an objective preference now. These sources do not identify a numerical jackpot sacrifice for Option 2 or a winning 2027 policy. Failure to resolve rare-event differences is not evidence of zero jackpot probability. Reach-20 is neither expected season best nor the chance of beating the eligible field. Keep the quantified policy trade pending; any later projection must state its model and dependence/calibration stress, with both requested objectives in natural units and no unexplained mixture weight.

**Cost if wrong:** sacrificing genuine jackpot chances, optimizing a proxy for the intended objective, or relying on fragile projections.

## D2. When is evidence strong enough to change how we pick?

**The situation.** The historical 86.2% headline is an actual-PA walk-forward average for 2024–2025 that used realized plate appearances. The published 2026 live record instead counts committed recommendations with linked graded contest slots: primary 92/125 (73.6%), double-down 69/102 (67.6%), April 1–September 13. Thirty-five committed selections without linked contest grades and twelve rows without commit evidence are excluded. These populations cannot establish model deterioration by subtraction.

**Evidence.**
- On the restricted 2026 diagnostic window, actual-PA oracle top picks scored 84.1%; geometric count normalization scored 65.1%, and the later diagnostic surfaces 66.7–71.4%. The count-normalized and estimated-PA walk-forward surfaces still use realized information; they are not archived pre-lock count forecasts. Reconstruction and served-score diagnostic argmax rates are not the full-season contest record. This bridge neither reproduces the historical headline nor assigns a cause to its gap.
- In that window, realized-count oracle information gives the largest displayed top-pick contrast. Stated minus realized is +0.031 on known candidate rows and +0.060 at native top picks, with different populations/weights; the fitted intercept departs from identity. These do not establish a uniform, stable offset or validate a correction. Ranking candidates within each day's slate was modest (within-slate AUC 0.566). Change over the window remains undetermined; drift, ball regime and luck were not identified as causes. The diagnostics are pointwise, exploratory and unadjusted.
- MLB's most-selected sheet covers a popularity-selected subset, about 9 of our roughly 250 daily candidates. All game links are inferred, not witnessed. On 63 headline dates, July 4–September 27, MLB's probabilities have lower Brier/log-loss point estimates. Pointwise, unadjusted difference intervals barely exclude zero when known no-PA counts as a miss and include zero when those rows are dropped. Both residual point estimates are positive, ours larger; MLB's no-PA-dropped residual interval includes zero. Its approximately 3 pp lower stated probabilities are a probability-level difference, not separate accuracy evidence. Ranking/top-pick differences include zero. The conditional association beyond ours is in-sample, not a prospective gain. The inspected sources did not define the forecast target, so this remains association under target sensitivities. The all-player sheet also carries the probability field for each player, but has no round id; its values were not read and game binding is unresolved.
- The May 1 field manifest has 310 members; 186 contributed to the comparison on 45 shared dates, May 1–July 3. Their pooled rate is 3,349/5,047 (66.4%); ours is 52/71 (73.2%), difference +6.9 pp [−6.4, +19.6]. Every shared-date quantity is conditional on both unverified name-based batch attribution and unchanged outcome bytes from the earlier accepted production build. The pooled rate weights prolific users and double-down days; it is not mean-user skill. The conditional interval includes zero and excludes attribution, acceptance, observation/missing-history and cross-date-dependence uncertainty. This is neither a skill ranking nor equivalence.
- Mechanism mining had no testable cell and nominated nothing: power-limited, not a negative mechanism result. Mechanism records were also not supplied, independently preventing nomination under its fifth condition. On 119 jointly resolved slots across 72 dates, fixed-cohort consensus hit 74.8% versus our 70.6%, difference +4.2 pp [−5.4, +12.8]. These retrospective, selected-cohort figures and public logs establish no pre-lock copying value.

**The choice.**
- **Option 1:** keep the current system pending fresh-season confirmation.
- **Option 2:** permit one named change after its registered, candidate-specific evidence and stress tests pass, accepting that its jackpot effect cannot be verified from this sample.

**Recommendation (reasoning):** Option 2, narrowly and with independent acceptance. A forecast change needs a frozen baseline/candidate comparison and earlier-fit/later-untouched 2027 validation, practical-effect/regression limits and applicable stress tests before affecting decisions. An operational repair instead needs its declared failure/recovery fixtures. A policy-only change needs its own state/eligibility replay and downstream-value gate; a forecast-score gate cannot substitute for it. There is no universal seed count or forecast gate.

If Eric selects calibration research, test identity versus one regularized intercept map, with a slope challenger only if supported, and held-out proper-score improvement. The 2026 read motivates testing this existing slot; it has nominated or validated no correction. A monotone map cannot improve ranking. Consistently remapping policy boundaries can leave behavior essentially unchanged; applying the map to old boundaries creates a separate policy change with its own gate. Nothing measured on 2026 is validation under the reserve decision.

**Cost if wrong:** better forecast scores but worse play, unjustified policy changes, or leaving a potentially useful correction untested.
