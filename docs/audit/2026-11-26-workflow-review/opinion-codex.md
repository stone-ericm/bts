## Wasted motion and the round limit

Ea's findings fell from 8 to 1, yet two rounds and two owner-directed text checks still ended in acceptance. Apply R64 at intake and require a concrete route to a wrong pick, wrong state, lost alert or lost day before a text finding consumes another round. G and F2 had duplicate first-round findings across reviewers. Count unique defect classes when diagnosing the process. Recurring clones, bundles, panes, delayed reviews after decisive stops and full gates on candidates about to be dropped add overhead. E's second gate was canceled before it ran and delayed no backtest. F1's first gate actually consumed slot time before cancellation.

The round limit works as a stop rule. In this window it often led to owner acceptance, especially for Ea and R52. F1b shows when the follow-up earns its keep. It changed the mechanism with a singleton lock and earned both reviews. Keep two rounds as a ceiling. Before spending round two on a repeated class, require a failing example and a proposed change that addresses the class. Use one follow-up when it can change the mechanism and demonstrate the fix. Otherwise bring Eric the remaining consequence, the evidence, and the choices. Batch decisions that can wait. Keep the coordinator across pieces and use the third counted BLOCK as a pause to diagnose, with a handoff when a fresh session has a specific purpose.
