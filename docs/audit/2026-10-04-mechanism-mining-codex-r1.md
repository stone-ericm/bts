## Findings

1. **P1 — Selection consistency is not the original protocol's manifest proof.** Amendment `A3`, lines 28–30, changes admission while saying the protocol is unchanged. The original requires at-lock/manifest-proven surfaces for primary top-N and variables 13–14 (`prereg.md:49–56,97–100,176–178`). Frozen W1.2 §2 explicitly says its identity/serialized-probability match does **not** bind the full slate to an attempt or final lock (`benchmark-bridge-design.md:21`). The writer saves before selection (`src/bts/orchestrator.py:276–280`), but overwrites the date file and omits feature values and an attempt identifier (`src/bts/slate.py:10–13,31–40,57–68`). Its timestamp and row count are not proof of the required universe, lineup assumptions, feature computation, and prediction time relative to the actual lock. A later attempt can preserve the selected row while changing other ranks. **Smallest fix:** qualify individual dates only with existing independent witnesses satisfying the original rule; otherwise raw rank/probability are null and their bins are `missing_surface`. An unproven served-slate diagnostic must be outside the protocol primary/FDR/nomination stream. No qualifying dates is an unavailable top-N result, not permission to weaken admission. The loader also currently accepts any supplied parquet without a provenance check (`leaderboard_backfilled_model_audit.py:101–115,178–237`).

2. **P1 — The proposed window drops unresolved units, and regeneration does not establish locked production or correct slot settlement.** A1 line 12 selects dates with a *resolved* slot, contrary to preserving void/pending/null production slots in the unit artifact (`prereg.md:43–45`). A date with only unresolved slots disappears. More fundamentally, `canonicalize_realized_picks.py:336–368` extracts any pick-shaped JSON with a probability and run time, without a delivery/lock/scoreability predicate. `canonicalize:370–408` labels outcomes from any hit on `(batter_id,date)`, ignores selected `game_pk` for grading and archived per-slot void settlement, and has no July 3 date-bound CLI (`:672–709`). A synthetic undelivered preview produced one row; a selected no-hit game plus a different same-date hit game produced a hit in the lookup. Production grading is game/slot-aware (`src/bts/picks.py:781–809,1003–1044`); the canonical producer cannot simply be called its equivalent. **Smallest fix:** an audited, explicitly bounded projection of authoritative locked production slots and slot labels, preserving unsupported resolution as unknown. Do not rewrite old canonical artifacts or infer delivery from file existence. Record the projection and upstream hashes. Dates with no production DD have no DD unit; dates with an unresolved DD do.

3. **P1 — The script can nominate despite contradictory all-tracked evidence and never enforces condition 5.** `decomposition_fdr_table:703–726` deletes n<15 cells before `_all_tracked_direction_not_contradicted` looks for a match; `:744–752` then treats missing support as passing with `fillna(True)`. A synthetic fixed cell with 30 deltas of +1 and its matching all-tracked cell with 10 deltas of −1 returned `actionable_mechanism_found=true`: the contradictory cell vanished. Removing all-tracked altogether also nominated. Matching missing-valued keys with `==` can additionally fail to find an otherwise corresponding cell (`:782–786`). Direction is not a significance test and does not inherit the n≥15 testability gate. Separately, `:747–752` checks only four conditions; `requires_lock_time_variables=true` at `:918` is metadata. The synthetic nomination required no availability witness. Canonical weather comes from the final PA frame (`canonicalize_realized_picks.py:169–205`), and the recipe `cutoff_iso` is not a prediction/lock timestamp. **Smallest fix:** evaluate direction from all matching resolved all-tracked cells before testability filtering; missing support is an explicit unknown, not evidence of passage. Require a stated feature mechanism and evidence that the variables it would use were available at lock, or mark condition 5 unknown/failed and prohibit an all-five nomination. This concerns the proposed mechanism's operational variables; it does not pretend the retrospective cohort was known live. Do not pool different cells to manufacture support.

4. **P1 — Unit retention and resolution are narrower/weaker than the protocol, and modal identity can be wrong.** `build_mechanism_units:467–470` returns empty when consensus is absent and otherwise uses an inner join. Four synthetic production rows with only three consensus rows yielded three artifact rows. A production `result_status=void, actual_hit=True` was marked resolved because the loader trusts non-null numeric `actual_hit`, and the unit predicate ignores status (`load_realized_production_picks:287–292`, `build_mechanism_units:512–516`). The void test only exercises `actual_hit=None`, so misses this case (`tests/scripts/test_leaderboard_mechanism_mining.py:283–323`). The consensus helper groups votes by `(batter_id,batter_name)` (`leaderboard_candidate_join_audit.py:260–276`): four users picking id 20 split over two name aliases lost to three users picking id 10 in a synthetic probe. Its majority rule over result strings turned conflicting `hit,hit,not_hit` labels into `hit` (`:280–287`), silently resolving an ambiguity, including possible same-batter/game ambiguities. **Smallest fix:** production-led date-slot retention per cohort, independent resolution/status masks, votes aggregated by id, and an explicit conservative settlement rule. Missing consensus is retained as unavailable; contradictory/unresolved settlement is not a majority-vote hit or miss. Validate hit indicators as binary. Neither outcomes nor missing support may cause a new modal winner to be selected.

5. **P1 — Legal pairs and tie sensitivity are not implemented, and A2 leaves consequential cases open.** `build_consensus:382–395` calls the two independent modal helpers and discards the candidate counts. There is no primary exclusion for DD, tie flag, or second FDR run. Freeze the constrained DD mode among ids other than the chosen primary, rather than an ambiguous "next modal" row. Count distinct users by id and retain the original slot vote denominator when the primary is excluded. Freeze ties at the *legal* maximum, including ties exposed by removing the primary. Flag a DD whose legality depends on a tied primary choice as tie-dependent as well; otherwise the sensitivity can retain consequences of the arbitrary primary tie. If no legal second id exists, or primary consensus is unavailable, retain the production DD unit with unavailable legal consensus. Do not duplicate the primary, silently drop the production unit, or substitute after seeing outcomes. Ordinary tied modes remain in the primary; remove their flagged units only in the sensitivity, without selecting a different tie winner. Run the rule separately for fixed and all-tracked cohorts. **Smallest fix:** specify these cases and implement them. Recompute the complete eligible family, q-values, and direction check for the fixed tie-excluded analysis. That analysis is a diagnostic, not a second opportunity to nominate a cell that failed the primary.

6. **P1 — Primary coverage currently includes unresolved slots and treats missing dates as top-N failures.** `summarize_units:599–607` computes coverage on `group`, not the protocol's resolved date-slots, and `fillna(False)` turns a missing surface into off-top-N. In the synthetic probe, a missing surface date lowered reported all-date top-1 to 2/3 while the two present dates gave 1; one present date was the void wrongly resolved in finding 4. Fixing status alone would still leave coverage using that void. **Smallest fix:** separate production resolution, consensus identity, joint outcome resolution, and surface admission masks. Primary coverage uses resolved production date-slots with a consensus id and a qualifying ranked surface; paired outcome estimands additionally require both known outcomes. Report all exclusion counts and denominator sizes. An absent qualifying surface produces unavailable coverage, whereas a batter absent from a complete qualifying ranking is a genuine top-N failure. Keep same-slot agreement as the stated fallback decision diagnostic. Never choose or rerank consensus based on resolution.

7. **P2 — Bootstrap defaults match, but the implementation changes the target/seed and extends the exact-test exception.** The CLI correctly defaults to block 7, 2,000 replicates, seed 20260510 (`mining.py:968–971`); do not report these defaults as wrong. However disagreement CIs use seed+1 (`:644`). The helper resamples equal-weight date means while reporting the unit-weighted mean (`candidate_join_audit.py:420–456`). For two +1 slots on one day and one −1 slot on the next, it reports mean 1/3 but resamples a distribution centered at 0; with the prescribed parameters the synthetic `p_mean_le_zero` was 0.9615. Resample day sums/counts together and compute the unit ratio. Also state that its geometric circular blocks follow ordered observed dates, not necessarily seven calendar days. The exact sign test is used for *every* cell (`mining.py:711`), while the protocol admits it as a small-count fallback (`prereg.md:120–125`). **Smallest fix:** explicitly declare the all-cell method extension if retaining it, rather than claiming everything is unchanged, and state its sign-exchangeability assumption. BY handles multiplicity dependence, not invalid cell p-values from serially dependent signs. Alternatively retain the original method restriction with a count-only, predeclared selector and a valid null-calibrated day-block test; do not feed the summary helper's uncentered tail probability into FDR. No outcome-driven choice between tests is allowed.

8. **P1 — X-22 is the right new exposure row, but "before any output is opened" is too late and "before any outcome is inspected" is false as a general claim.** `build_audit:817–838` loads outcome-bearing production/public inputs before writing a report. Canonical regeneration reads PA outcomes and *unconditionally* prints regime hit counts/rates, even without `--summary` (`canonicalize_realized_picks.py:370–408,701–709`). X-22 must precede those executions, conversions, and joins, not merely their final file opening. The exposure register already records an overlapping July 3 retrospective own-vs-tracked outcome read in X-10 (`docs/audit/2026-09-22-exposure-register.md:20`); a new registration cannot make 2026 untouched. The existing X-21 bridge registration does not cover mining and nomination. **Smallest fix:** publish X-22 after freezing design/code choices but before any new outcome-bearing execution; include the source projection and both prespecified analysis streams, isolate outputs until completion, suppress intermediate outcome summaries, and disclose prior overlap. Review code and synthetic inputs first. Failed technical runs may be retried at identical pins without results inspection; changing dates/cohort/method after inspection is exploration. The review itself performed no new real outcome read.

9. **P2 — Several promised report products are absent or overclaimed.** The FDR table only emits n≥15 cells, so the promised sparse-cell inventory does not exist (`mining.py:704–706`). It has a BY boolean but no required BH-only/exploratory designation when BY fails. There is no conditional miscalibration calculation: probability bins enter grouping, but mean predicted probability/residual is not reported (`summarize_units:613–649`, `decomposition_fdr_table:712–722`; original secondary estimand at `prereg.md:68–71`). The ranked loader requires parquet with `actual_hit`, whereas the proposed `bts_slate_v1` inputs are JSON without outcomes (`backfilled_model_audit.py:38–39,101–115`; `src/bts/slate.py:35–40,54–64`). Its best-rank collapse across `(date,batter_id)` selects a probability from one of possibly multiple games (`:209–216`): "appears anywhere in top-N" remains a batter-coverage diagnostic, but that probability cannot silently be paired to an unidentified public game's settlement. Finally `pre_registration.status` is hardcoded, paths are recorded without input hashes, and neither amendment nor X-22 is bound (`mining.py:860–879`). **Smallest fix:** the bounded adapter/report changes below; unsupported secondary measurements and availability stay unavailable, not invented.
