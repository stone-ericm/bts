## Why
The Streak Saver flag (`account_state/saver_state.json`: `not_earned`, `active` or `used`, for one season; anything else reads as `uninitialized`) is the only authority for the saver in the live decision. Four things are wrong on `main`:
1. The decision passes `state == "active"` to the policy. A saver that is not earned yet (`not_earned`, streak below 10) therefore reaches the policy as "spent", and the tracked policy chooses differently on that, mostly at streaks 5 to 9. In the policy's own model the saver bit means "not yet consumed", which is true below 10.
2. The decision, the health check and the dashboard pick the flag's season from the contest observation's `source_date`. At the start of a season that date is last season's, so last season's flag (an `active` saver, say) drives this season's first decisions, and a dashboard click writes to last season's flag.
3. A flag that contradicts the streak (`not_earned` at streak 10 to 15, or `uninitialized`) runs conservative with at most a log line.
4. `bts saver-state --use` exits 0 and does nothing when the flag is `not_earned`, which is exactly the state in which the operator needs it (a saver earned and consumed between two fetches). The next fetch then promotes the flag to `active`.

## Definitions
- **Y, the current season:** the calendar year, in `America/New_York`, of the caller's `now`. Every site below already has a `now` or takes the wall clock; none may derive the season from `source_date` any more.
- **The policy saver** for flag state `s` at decision streak `n`: `active` → `True`; `used` → `False`; `uninitialized` → `False`; `not_earned` → `True` when `n < 10`, else `False`.
