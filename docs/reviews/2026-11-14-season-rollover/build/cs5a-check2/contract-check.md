## Limits

- **NOTE.** R33's automatic rule allows the low-streak double to go out near 57 after fetch, saver edit, reactivation, season change when an old date is explicitly targeted, or expiry of a dated override. Drift can also turn what should be a skip into a send. Dated matching stops when the in-effect tuple changes or expires. Restoring the same tuple restores its match.
