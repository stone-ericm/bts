1. **F4, a false `CRITICAL` from the contest-state health check on Opening Night and the day after.** The end-of-day health run sees a settled 2027 pick before the overnight fetch has a `source_date`. Reproduce with `health.contest_state.check(..., expected=True)` in the states the audit names, for BOTH an automatic observation and a day run on a dated observation (`bts set-contest-streak --date`), which is how the opening days will actually be run. Bound: a fix may only lower a `CRITICAL` that is false in exactly those states to `INFO`; it must not hide the alert when the automatic reading really has no `source_date` for more than the expected overnight lag, and it must not touch the live refusal the check appends last (change set 4b-2).
2. **F5, park drag.** (a) `park_drag_freshness` pages `CRITICAL` on Opening Night because March is not in its offseason months and the table's last game is last September. (b) `park_drag_producer.refresh()` crashes on an empty reseeded store (`NaN` year, `NaT` date). Bound: (a) no freshness alarm for a gap that is only the offseason, and the alarm back in force once this season's games are in the table's reach (say exactly from when); (b) an empty store refreshes without raising and bootstraps after the first game. Do not change what the feature computes for a non-empty store (prove it on a fixture).
3. **F6, the fetch cron in research mode.** `scripts/cron-setup-hetzner.sh` installs the four `fetch-contest-streak` lines with `--dm-recipient` under either intent, so a box restored in research mode with last season's cookies DMs an auth failure every six hours. Bound: under `entry_intent = "research"` the four lines are still installed (the readings are wanted in the ledger) but WITHOUT `--dm-recipient`, so a failure is logged and not DM'd; under `"enter"` the lines are exactly as today. Check what `bts fetch-contest-streak` does with no recipient on each failure kind (it must still exit non-zero and write its status). **Do NOT change the handle in `deploy/systemd/bts-leaderboard.service`:** which Bluesky handle is right is the owner's to say; list it in the handover as an open question.
4. **F7, the live-forward capture on a nine-batter Opening Night lock.** When the early lock sees only one team's lineup, the capture exports 9 rows where its manifest says 10, fails its own verification, and never re-exports, so the capture is wedged and alarms for good. Bound: a capture whose slate had fewer scored batters than `top_n` records the effective count and verifies against it; a capture whose verification failed is exported again before any outcome is known, never after. Nothing about the PICK changes (ruling R18 ledgers that the early lock sees only posted lineups). The capture's frozen-evidence rules still hold: do not weaken any check that guards against reading outcomes.
5. **F8, last season's files pooled into this season's shadow statistics** (`skip_policy_shadow` and `shadow_eval` glob every year). Bound: both read only the current season's files by default (the ET year of now, or of the date being processed where one is passed), with last season still readable when asked for explicitly. Say which commands change their output on the existing 2026 files and show it is only the pooling.
