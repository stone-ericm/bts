# Change set 5b of the season-boundary build: choosing again after a late or corrected observation (draft 1)

**Tier A** (the operator's instructions on the pick path; no selection or delivery code changes). Base: `main` at the commit named in the author's brief (it contains change sets 4a to 6a and 5a). One commit on branch `sb-cs5b`.

## Why
Since 5a, a saved pick chosen on other state is refused at delivery, and a refusal chooses nothing: "a later scheduled check or fallback refresh may choose a replacement, if one remains". When the operator supplies or corrects the day's dated observation AFTER the last scheduled check, nothing remains, and the day ends with no pick although a valid state is now on disk. Ruling R33 named this change set as "a way to make the scheduler run a check on demand".

**Design (ruling R34, the lead's): no new wake mechanism.** The scheduler already has one, exercised by every deploy: when it is restarted, `run_day` plans the day's checks again and `plan_run_times` never drops a REGULAR check whose time has passed, so the main loop runs it at once (`if now < target` is false), with an empty `confirmed_sides`, on the state now on disk. This change set (a) proves, through the real `run_day`, exactly what a restart does in the late-observation cases, (b) tells the operator to do it, at the three places the operator looks, and (c) writes the procedure down. If (a) shows that a restart does NOT choose again in a case below, the author STOPS and reports: do not add a mechanism.

Cost if wrong (ledgered): the operator needs a shell on the box (they have one: the observation is entered there); a restart is slower than a signal by the schedule fetch; a restart re-runs every passed regular check until one locks, as any restart does today.

## Definitions
- **The restart line** is exactly: `To choose again now on the live state, restart the scheduler: systemctl --user restart bts-scheduler`
- **Today's saved pick** (for the status display only): the production pick file for the ET date of `now` in the picks directory, read with `picks.load_pick`.

## Known limits (state them in the handover; they are not findings)
- A restart takes a schedule fetch and one prediction cascade before anything is sent; inside the last `5 + cascade budget` minutes before first pitch it cannot deliver in time, and the cutoff guard refuses.
- A restart re-runs each passed regular check in turn until one locks (existing behaviour); on a skip day that is several cascades.
- A restart during result polling interrupts it (existing behaviour of every deploy); the late-observation case arises before the day's pick is locked.
- The two lines of row 2 are printed even when a later check remains and no restart is needed.
- Nothing restarts the scheduler automatically.
