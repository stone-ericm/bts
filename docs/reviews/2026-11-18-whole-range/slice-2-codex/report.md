# Verdict

**DEPLOYABLE AFTER THESE FIXES**

## Blocking findings

1. **The policy loses the last playable day and can stop a reachable streak a week earlier.** With the bundled policy, an automatic streak of 56, a 96% candidate and three playable games on September 26, HEAD wrote an MDP skip. Its DM called 96% below the approximate 80% bar. The next morning's `day-outcome` returned `deliberate_skip`, exit 0, with no DM. September 20 at streak 43 and trusted best 56 also skipped because the lookup counted six days and the tail stop rule capped the reachable streak at 55. Counting today in both replays gave one day and a single that scored 57 on September 26, and seven days and a double that scored 45 on September 20. The date named as the last regular-season day at `src/bts/strategy.py:46` is subtracted exclusively at `src/bts/strategy.py:241`, and `src/bts/simulate/mdp.py:187` skips at zero. **Smallest fix:** make the shared day-count helper inclusive on the configured last date while keeping zero for dates after it. [Bundled policy replay](matrix-policy-head.log) · [Last-day control](matrix-policy-last-control.log) · [Reachability control](matrix-policy-boundary-control.log)

2. **The automatic rollback target cannot serve 2027 and can invalidate the next forward start.** The workflow restores only the SHA and dependencies at `.github/workflows/deploy.yml:169`. After a healthy HEAD send, the BASE CLI recovered the same commit without resending, but on a fresh playable day it became ready, sent a skip advisory and wrote a deliberate MDP skip. BASE still uses the 2026 date at `base/src/bts/strategy.py:42`. A BASE scorer rewrote the HEAD streak without `season` or `applied_dates`, and a pre-applied but unfinalized hit went from 13 to 14 on retry (`base/src/bts/picks.py:532`, `:566`). HEAD read both rewritten 2027 streaks as zero when scoped. BASE's next successful fetch overwrote automatic v2 with v1 (`base/src/bts/cli.py:2026`), so HEAD refused live state with the original switch still active (`src/bts/season_contract.py:128`). The HEAD cron table also retains a command absent from BASE. **Smallest fix:** require a tested rollback revision that can serve 2027 with this data and crontab. Until it exists, keep outward scheduling and state-writing cron off on a failed live deploy and page the operator for recovery. Before re-enabling HEAD after any BASE run, replay or reconcile the local streak as needed and run a switched HEAD fetch. [Rollback replay](deploy-rollback.log) · [Forward read](deploy-forwarded.log) · [Fetch downgrade](deploy-old-fetch.log) · [Live refusal](deploy-new-read.log)

3. **A failed sync or initial restart bypasses the only rollback branch.** The actual workflow shell starts with `set -e`. After resetting to the candidate at `.github/workflows/deploy.yml:105`, the commands at `:106` and `:115` can exit before the canary or rollback runs. Shimming either command to return 1 left the candidate SHA checked out, with no second reset or restart. A dashboard 503 control reached the rollback and restored BASE. The real half-configured CLI exits 2 before READY, so a failed initial restart is relevant to this deploy. **Smallest fix:** route sync and initial restart failures through the same recovery branch as a failed canary, subject to the live rollback constraint above. [CLI startup](deploy-startup.log) · [Workflow shell](deploy-workflow.log)

4. **The new day-outcome DM can name an unselected second batter.** The July 15 single sent Batter 101 alone. Its committed `decision.json` had `action=single` and put runner-up Batter 102 in `double_down` via `src/bts/scheduler.py:904`. With no qualifying receipts, the 08:30 command sent “Pick: Batter 101 + Batter 102.” A positive sealed receipt for Batter 101 alone correctly yielded `entered` but printed that same pair. **Smallest fix:** in `src/bts/day_outcome.py:46` render the second name only for a double, covering existing records. Write only the executed leg to `double_down` on future commits and keep runner-up metadata in `second_candidate`. [Single with no receipt](matrix-policy-head.log) · [Single with a positive receipt](cron.log)

## Findings that need an owner decision

- **Define the minimum pre-cutoff entry-check coverage.** At a 14:54 restart for a 15:00 game, the real scheduler sent the pick with one minute left. The real checker saw no pick at the 14:45 tick and exited outside its window at 15:00. The first alert was next morning's `entry_unverifiable`. This is a property of the retained 15-minute cadence and the five-minute cutoff. Decide whether the short-window send may rely on the operator alone. [Cron and restart replay](cron.log)
- **Decide what the deploy canary should certify.** Both required flags with an absent switch and no dated observation reached READY, paged, and idled after refusing every pick. The workflow shell returned success when the shims reported both services active and HTTP 200. This is expected useful liveness during manual setup, but leaves the same green signal if an automatic in-season box has lost its state. A live-read check at activation or deploy needs to distinguish those two cases. [CLI startup](deploy-startup.log) · [Canary replay](deploy-workflow.log)
