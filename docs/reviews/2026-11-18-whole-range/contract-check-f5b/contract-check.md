## 2. Early-only state

- `watchdog_sequences.py` and `round_one.py`: a 09:45 wrong-game alert followed by a verified manual fix at 09:50 gets an all-clear on round 1. F5b sends none, and 10:00 is already at cutoff. If both ledgers send the initial warning, they also send two identical all-clears. Each independently reads the account. A 05:00 early confirmation for game 101 also skips the 05:15 check after a same-date replacement with game 102 at 06:10. A manual check then finds the wrong game. Date alone also carries spent tiers across early-only replacements.
- `health_cases.py` and `standing.py`: a manual `present_unverified` live marker for D hides D's early `dm_failed`. After a manual confirmation, the next date's original-hours write displaces the live recovery and revives D's early failure. Conversely, a Jan 1 early confirmation erases Dec 31's unresolved early state before delayed health. Midnight without a new pick leaves both files alone. Restoring Dec 31's marker brings the old alert back and January never treats it as terminal.
- Interrupted send before replace leaves the previous marker and retries. Interruption after the confirming replace can lose the all-clear and leave the next early check terminal. That ordering is inherited from the base. Early state remains invisible to normal live runs, and manual checks bypass the installed lock.

## 4. Health

Ran the paired marker matrix with and without early data. Matching live statuses win, including unknown and partial objects. A live list, null or scalar raises `AttributeError`, invalid UTF-8 raises `UnicodeDecodeError`, and a writerless live FIFO blocks on both trees. Missing, malformed-JSON and directory live paths are quiet on the base and take the scratch fallback. A stale live date allows fallback. Early missing, bad JSON, wrong date, bad encoding, oversized, invalid tiers, non-object, FIFO and directory inputs are quiet. An early alert before cutoff stays quiet. After fallback, the inherited cutoff reader still blocks on a FIFO pick and raises on list or invalid UTF-8 picks. The early command's own marker read is safe, but its atomic replace over a directory raises after sending the DM.
