## Six answers

1. **Q1 / R-D1 — MUST FIX.** On v252, `ExecCondition=` exit 0 (or a status in `SuccessExitStatus=`) continues. Exit 1–254 otherwise skips the remaining start commands without marking a failure. Exit 255 or abnormal termination (signal, timeout) fails the service. In v252 `src/core/service.c`, `service_sigchld_event()` maps a normal condition refusal to `SERVICE_SKIP_CONDITION`, `service_enter_dead()` leaves it dead/inactive, and `service_shall_restart()` excludes that result from `Restart=always`. A skipped condition can run `ExecStopPost=` if configured. The shipped unit has none. This covers boot, dependency, explicit restart and an automatic start that reaches the condition. It cannot stop an already-running service, prevent an earlier spawn failure such as a missing required `EnvironmentFile`, or guard a different unit. `/etc/systemd/user/<unit>.d/` is a user-manager drop-in path. Same-named higher-priority user drop-ins and later assignments can replace/reset conditions. Verify the effective `ExecCondition` command and flags after reload, `NeedDaemonReload=no`, and the actual loaded unit and dependencies. Test in Linux v252 along with the shell shims. [v252 service manual](https://github.com/systemd/systemd/blob/v252/man/systemd.service.xml), [v252 service implementation](https://github.com/systemd/systemd/blob/v252/src/core/service.c), [v252 user unit paths and drop-ins](https://github.com/systemd/systemd/blob/v252/man/systemd.unit.xml)
2. **Q2 — NOTE for ordering, MUST FIX for lifetime.** An actual writer forked between arm and drain returned 75. A writer admitted before arm blocked EX. A double-forked, detached grandchild retained SH after the launcher exited and blocked EX until its FD closed. EX proves inherited shared descriptors are closed. A descendant can outlive its last descriptor. Preserve shared-before-hold and a single stable inode. Define a supported writer as foreground, or supervise its entire process group and descendants. A job may retain the descriptor indefinitely, or close it and keep writing. Timeout must leave containment intact. The regular 01:00 grader may run until ~06:00 ET. [Flock probe](r3-review-probe.log), [installed job template](../../scripts/cron-setup-hetzner.sh#L91)
3. **Q3 — MUST FIX.** Same-boot is necessary for any provisional capability and insufficient for ownership. The arm→stop, lease-record→timer, expiry→unlink and release→trap races in the model can leave writers running, clear a failed hold, publish a late success or re-hold a success. Use a boot-relative deadline, serialize expiry/fail/release with ownership and comparison under one lock, and give an independent local supervisor a bounded stop path through the transaction. Keep the actual scheduler condition closed for the full hold. [Hold model results](r3-lease-probe.log)

Rehearse with the restored cache, abort held and page if out of budget, and leave enough time before the next lineup check. Suppressing successful samples can still trip missed-ping grace. Qualify normal deploys against the actual integration, with period + grace beyond the maximum healthy suppression plus two five-minute tick intervals (35m for a 25m bound). Keep an independent box-liveness check.
