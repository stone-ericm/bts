## 1. Rule arithmetic

- `arithmetic`, `edge-rates`, `multi-bin` logs. Loaded the shipped, SHA-paired NPZ. `p=0.7336111111111111`, `pb=0.5511111111111111`. Binary64 gave double at 1–3 and single at 4–180. The only shipped ties are maximal single/skip at every day 116–180. Single wins all 65.
- At current best `s`, the terminal miss leaves `s`, a single hit leaves `s+1+g`, and a double hit leaves `s+2+g`. Subtracting `s` gives the recurrence. Every subsequent step evaluates the same nondecreasing play functions at a gain at least as large as before. The previous winning play therefore still meets or exceeds skip, including with float rounding. Day 1 also has a play maximizer. The bounded loop finishes for all valid one-bin rates.
- Validated, saved and loaded one-bin `(p,pb)` of `(0,0)`, `(.75,0)`, `(1,.75)`, `(1,1)`. They give all singles by ties, all singles, two doubles then singles, and all doubles, respectively. Using only bin 0 silently ignores the rest. A one-bin approximation cannot optimize the whole season after a reset or under changing availability or quality.
