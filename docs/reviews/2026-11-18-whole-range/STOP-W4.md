## Why it stopped (the check's four MUST FIX items)
1. **The trigger takes the day from a viable check.** Version 1 fired whenever the next check was after the saved pick's deadline; a check before the saved slot's cutoff can still choose a better pick (reproduced above), and a 03:00 preview with a raised budget floor triggers before the morning's first check. Fixable in the contract: protect a check strictly before the cutoff, require a previously live-selected candidate (a preview alone never qualifies).
2. **The actions read the wrong file or the wrong way.** The definition filtered out delivered, attempted and expired files before the wake re-read could classify them; a new attempt plus a private commit at wake would rewrite the commit. `lstat` then `load_pick` can still open a FIFO swapped in between; `load_decision` conflates unreadable with absent.
3. **A re-plan before unchanged helpers does not remove the stale-state failures inside them.** Shown by replay: a send started at 19:05:01 after a two-second marker write at 19:04:59; a defer planned at 18:42:59 still removed the live file after the window closed and unlinked a newly written attempt marker; an empty W4 refresh plus a failed status read sent a postponed candidate where `main` sent nothing. The smallest safe version needs guarded actions: archive staged and removal committed as one guarded operation with a fresh feasibility check, competing same-slate writers serialized, the status result kept through the re-plan, unknown status never authorizing a NEW send on this path, and a post-marker time check before transport.
4. **The pending missed-pick check needs a durable owner.** Recovery from the last `fallback_refreshes` entry repages after a late or void refusal, loses the archive reference on a crash between removal and save, and depends on a dispatcher whose same-day dedup can let a failed `missed_pick` erase another source's sent record or suppress a different slate. The smallest safe version is an explicit per-slate obligation (scheduled, in progress, pending, resolved) persisted before any wait or removal, with its own receipt separate from the shared status file.

## Today on `main` (W4 open)
When a lineup check returns no pick and the next planned check is after an earlier saved pick's cutoff, the day ends with no fallback, no pick, no missed-pick DM and no decision; the saved pick is archived as `stale_pick_*` by the later check. It needs two or more checks left in the day, the last check before the cutoff to return nothing (no predictions, a cascade error, a contest-state refusal), and a saved pick from an earlier check or run. A one-game day (or the day's last check) is not affected: the post-loop final fallback delivers.
