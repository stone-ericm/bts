## Why
`docs/audit/2026-11-18-proptest-trial/README.md`: a property-testing trial found the defect. The exact-date merge in the "Team bullpen composite" block of `src/bts/features/compute.py` makes the availability of `opp_bullpen_hr_30g` depend on whether the pitching team has any plate appearance classified as relief on D. In the 2025-only replay this left the feature missing on 30 of 4,493 eligible team-dates (941 PAs, 0.517% of all PAs). They comprise 25 complete games and five game-sides with a missing probable pitcher. Their hit rate was 0.1424 versus 0.2211 on old-valued rows. The function's contract (its docstring; `CLAUDE.md` "Safety Rules") is that a feature for date D uses only rows dated strictly before D. The fix removes this availability signal. The existing morning lookup at serving can still be stale or missing. Newly computed feature values can affect live scores directly through the bullpen lookup and indirectly through fresh model fits. Historical baselines remain evidence of their original computation and data coverage.

## The rule
D is the PA's MLB `officialDate` as stored in the processed data and parsed by the existing `pd.to_datetime` path. Supported timestamps represent the same timezone-naive midnight date key as date strings. The official-date guarantee applies to this input convention. Use the parsed key directly and preserve the returned date column. Use the fielding-side MLB team ID from the game lookup as the identity. A new ID starts a new history. History spans seasons for the same ID.

Keep the existing historical PA classification: a known probable on that side of that game and a different PA pitcher ID. Pool all qualifying PAs for the same team ID and official date before dividing `sum(is_hit)` by `count(is_hit)`. Both games of a doubleheader share one bucket and one output value, independent of input order or game number. Game 2 cannot use game 1 as history. Preserve the pipeline's official-date assignment of suspended and resumed PAs.

At D, consider the last 30 reliever-PA date buckets strictly before D. Weight defined daily rates equally and require at least 10 defined rates in those 30 buckets. A zero-count bucket has an undefined rate, occupies a window slot and contributes neither a rate nor a valid observation. A no-relief date consumes no slot. Match the most recent eligible history position even when that position produces `NaN`. Use no PA information from D or later **official dates**. A known team with an unknown probable on D gets its ordinary prior-history value. Unknown team, missing date or insufficient history gives `NaN`. With an empty probable lookup, emit the all-`NaN` feature and retain the base output schema, including the absence of `opp_pitching_team_id`.

## Known limits (state them in the handover; they are not findings)
- The fix repairs how a value ATTACHES to date D. It leaves the daily reliever rates themselves as computed today: an opener listed as the probable, a probable who did not start, or a missing probable still skew or suppress which plate appearances count as relief on that date and so what later windows contain.
- Suspended games: resumed plate appearances keep their original official date (`build.py`), so a window can contain events that happened a day later than their date key. Unchanged.
- Serving still lags: after a reliever date the morning lookup returns a value that excludes that date's results (the existing `dropna().last()` staleness, the M3 audit). The fix improves parity at gaps and leaves this.
- The model's training inputs change on about half a percent of rows, so a retrained model differs slightly; no shipped artifact is rebuilt here; fresh probabilities can change policy actions at fixed bins; backtest baselines, saved profiles and audit reports computed before this change describe the old computation.
- Daily production blend pickles are keyed by date only, so a cached pickle from before the change holds the old computation.
