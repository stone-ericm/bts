## Notes
1. **`O_NONBLOCK` left set on `fd` passed to `io.TextIOWrapper` (`src/bts/health/alert.py:111, 123`):** On local POSIX filesystems (Linux ext4/XFS/tmpfs, macOS APFS) and standard NFS, `O_NONBLOCK` has no effect on verified `S_IFREG` descriptors (Linux removed `-o mand` mandatory locking in 5.15; macOS has none; `BufferedReader`/`BufferedWriter` loop across multi-chunk I/O and flush on `with`-exit, verified at 8 MiB). CPython's `TextIOWrapper`, however, assumes a blocking descriptor: if a custom FUSE/HSM mount ever honored `O_NONBLOCK` on `S_IFREG` and returned `EAGAIN`, `write()` raises `BlockingIOError` (`OSError`, caught and logged), whereas `read()` gets `None` from `FileIO`/`BufferedReader` and raises uncaught `TypeError` at 0 bytes (`probe_item1_races_and_stress.py`). Calling `os.set_blocking(fd, True)` after `S_ISREG` would guard even non-local FUSE mounts.
2. **Test-only `O_TRUNC` gap and minor test items (`tests/health/test_alert_status_cs6b2_review.py:161-174, 192-198`; `tests/health/test_alert_status_cs6b2.py:131, 204`):** `test_nothing_is_truncated_before_the_type_is_known` spies on `os.ftruncate` but not on `os.O_TRUNC` in `os.open` (mutating `_write_regular_file_text` to pass `O_TRUNC` and drop `ftruncate` passes `review.py` `15/15`; caught by `adopt/test_alert_status_cs6b2_r2_adopt.py`, which also covers symlink-to-FIFO and missing-to-FIFO swaps). In `test_no_descriptor_is_left_open`, `seen["reader"]` is closed outside `try/finally` if `dispatch` raises; in `test_alert_status_cs6b2.py`, line 131 retains an unused `with monkeypatch.context() as read:` block, and line 204 adds a third beneficial hunk (`cwd=Path(__file__).resolve().parents[2]` on `git archive`, fixing Carbon's round-1 Note 3).

## Findings 1 and 2
- **Finding 1 (reader `stat()`-to-open race) reproduced on `38d707bd` and closed on `a50d439f` (`src/bts/health/alert.py:117-127, 209`):** Swapping a regular status file to a FIFO after the reader's `Path.stat()` in `CRITICAL`, empty-list, and no-recipient modes blocked `38d707bd` at the 0.35s `SIGALRM` with 0 DMs when the FIFO had no peer, an open writer holding covering `sent` JSON bytes, or a reader only; when a writer wrote covering JSON and closed after 40ms, `38d707bd` consumed the pipe bytes and wrongly suppressed `CRITICAL`. On `HEAD`, `_read_regular_file_text` opens with `O_RDONLY | O_NONBLOCK` and checks `stat.S_ISREG(os.fstat(fd).st_mode)` before constructing `TextIOWrapper`: all 12 cases completed in `0.2–0.4ms`, left preloaded pipe bytes unread (`81B`), treated the snapshot as `None`, and sent `CRITICAL`.
- **Finding 2 (writer `stat()`-to-open race) reproduced on `38d707bd` and closed on `a50d439f` (`src/bts/health/alert.py:88, 97-115`):** Swapping to a FIFO after `_persist_health_dm_delivery_status`'s `stat()` (and creating a FIFO after `mkdir` when `stat()` saw `FileNotFoundError`) made `38d707bd` block at 0.35s after sending `CRITICAL` (or on `no_recipient`) when no reader existed, and silently write `430–452B` of status JSON into the pipe with 0 warnings when a reader existed. On `HEAD`, `_write_regular_file_text` opens with `O_WRONLY | O_CREAT | O_NONBLOCK` (no `O_TRUNC`), checks `S_ISREG(os.fstat(fd).st_mode)` before `os.ftruncate(fd, 0)`, raises `OSError` (`ENXIO` without reader, `"not a regular file"` with reader), writes `0B` to the pipe, and logs the persistence warning in `0.2–0.8ms`.
- **Stress checks on `HEAD`:**
  - *Type change between `os.open` and `os.fstat`:* Impossible on an open descriptor in POSIX (`fd` references an open file description bound to an inode whose `st_mode & S_IFMT` is fixed for the inode's lifetime; unlinking/replacing the path rebinds the directory entry to a new inode). Verified empirically by swapping a FIFO onto `status_path` inside an `os.fstat` wrapper: `fstat(fd)` still saw `S_ISREG`, read/wrote the original regular inode (confirmed via hardlink), and ignored the FIFO.
  - *Symlinks to special files:* All 21 stable and `stat->open` race cases for 1-hop and 2-hop symlinks to FIFOs (no peer, writer with bytes, reader) and `/dev/null` finished without blocking, read/wrote `0B` on the special target, and logged the write warning.
  - *Descriptor leaks:* Across 14 failure/execution paths × 10,000 calls each (`140,000` calls: `ENOENT`, `EACCES`, injected `fstat` `EIO` on read/write, non-regular FIFO/dir/`/dev/null`, `UnicodeDecodeError`, `JSONDecodeError`, `ENXIO`, injected `ftruncate` `EIO`, `ENOSPC` in `write`, `UnicodeEncodeError`), `/dev/fd` stayed at `6` before and after (`0` leaked fds).
  - *Locks, concurrent writers, BSD flags:* Regular files held open `O_RDWR` by a child process with `flock(LOCK_EX)`, POSIX `fcntl(F_SETLK, F_WRLCK)`, or macOS `O_EXLOCK` read the prior banner and persisted `sent` without blocking; BSD `UF_IMMUTABLE` and `UF_APPEND` read cleanly, sent the DM, and logged `Operation not permitted`.
