## 1. Rule arithmetic

- `arithmetic.log` reads the SHA-paired shipped NPZ and iterates with binary64 operations in item 2's order. Rates are `0.7336111111111111` and `0.5511111111111111`. Double wins on days 1–3, single on 4–180. The only equalities are single/skip on days 116–180 inclusive, all resolved to single. Item 2's iteration and tie order are correct.
- At current best `s`, a miss leaves `s` and a hit leads to `s+1+g` or `s+2+g`. Subtracting `s` yields the same gain for every `s` under the stated model. At day 1 some play meets zero. The maximum of the two play values is a nondecreasing function of `g`, including under binary64 rounding. Since `g` never decreases, that maximum at each step is at least the previous gain. The play-first tie order therefore excludes skip. Finite rates `0 <= pb <= p <= 1` bound the gain by `2*d` and the loop runs exactly `d` steps. Saved, validated and reloaded `(p,pb)` of `(0,0)`, `(.75,0)`, `(1,.75)` and `(1,1)` yielded respectively 180 singles, 180 singles, two doubles then singles, and 180 doubles. A valid two-bin NPZ loaded and took the scratch forced single through the bin-count check.
