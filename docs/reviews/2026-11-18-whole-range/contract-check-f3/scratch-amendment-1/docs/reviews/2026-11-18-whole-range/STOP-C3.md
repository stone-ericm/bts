## What was being fixed (W14 / W23, still open on `main`)
`picks._replay_season_streak` returns "unavailable" as soon as it meets a past pick file with no terminal result. A refused 03:00 preview, or any pick file left behind that was never committed, therefore disables the nightly rebuild of `streak.json` (the MODEL's streak and saver; the contest streak is separate and unaffected) for the rest of the season, and `bts reconcile` says only `No scoring changes detected`.

## Why both designs failed
- **Design 1 (skip a past pick with no evidence of a commit):** an ENTERED pick can leave no evidence (the DM is accepted and the acknowledgement lost, then the attempt marker is cleared; a private-mode interrupt between the lock and the decision; `bts run` posts and is interrupted before the marker). The replay then ran, dropped the entered day and saved a wrong streak (0 where 10 was right), where the base refused to replay and kept the streak.
- **Design 2 (skip only on positive evidence: a non-scoreable decision, or the preview's stamp, with no delivery signal and no scheduler lock):** the positive evidence can be wrong too. A scheduler restart rewrites the day's state unlocked after a private lock; the end-of-day skip record is written from an earlier skip candidate after an accepted send whose acknowledgement was lost; a non-live `orchestrate` stamps a fresh choice exactly as a preview does. Same wrong save. The guarded correction reader also refused a terminal pick with a numeric-string id that the base graded.
- **The underlying fact:** the files on disk cannot prove that a pick was NOT entered. Every writer (scheduler send, private lock, `bts run`, `orchestrate`, end-of-day skip) has a window between the outward act and the record of it. Until those windows are closed (W11: keep the attempt marker on an ambiguous response; write an attempt before a manual or orchestrated post), no rule that skips an unresolved pick automatically is safe.
