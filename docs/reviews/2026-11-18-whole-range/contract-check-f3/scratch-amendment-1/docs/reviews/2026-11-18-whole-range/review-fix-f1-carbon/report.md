## Edits
1. **`docs/reviews/2026-11-18-whole-range/kickoff-fix-f1.md` § A6.4 (line 115)** — A6.4 states that on rollback the base sends its all-clear when overwriting a wrong-game marker, which holds for `alerted`/`wrong_game` (with a DM recipient) but not for `dm_failed`/`wrong_game` or `present_unverified` carrying `escalations: ["wrong_game"]` without `"initial"`, where the base overwrites the marker with `confirmed` quietly and stops checking (`probe_04_summary.json`). Replace item 4 with:
   > 4. A rollback to the base: on a batter match, the base overwrites a non-terminal wrong-game marker (`alerted`, `dm_failed`, or `present_unverified` with `wrong_game` in `escalations`) with `confirmed`, sends its all-clear only when the marker was `alerted` or listed `initial`, and stops checking. Treat a confirmation from rolled-back code as batter-only.
2. **`docs/reviews/2026-11-18-whole-range/kickoff-fix-f1.md` § A6.5 (line 116)** — Three details of unchanged `bts day-outcome` are stated incompletely (`probe_04_summary.json`): (i) `day-outcome` ranks receipts without timestamp order and ignores `marker_status`/`exit_code`, so a *later* `unit_unverified` receipt sealed on an unresolved M9 re-check also outranks an *earlier* `wrong_game` (or `no_pick`/`mismatch`) receipt and yields `entered_game_unverified` (exit 0, no 08:30 DM, whereas the base stopped after confirming at the first wrong-game check and sent `entry_unverifiable` or `not_entered` at 08:30); (ii) `entered_game_unverified` requires every slot to be `confirmed` or `unit_unverified` (`wrong_round`, `ambiguous`, and `unsupported_duplicate_selection` confirm in the watchdog without a prior wrong-game warning, yet read `not_entered` or `entry_unverifiable` at 08:30); (iii) a bound `mismatch` receipt also counts as observed absence (`not_entered`). Replace item 5 with:
   > 5. The morning `bts day-outcome` is unchanged. It reads the sealed receipts by priority regardless of order and ignoring `marker_status`: timely game proof (`entered`), timely `confirmed`/`unit_unverified` batter match (`entered_game_unverified`), late game proof (`entered_late`), observed absence (`no_pick` or `mismatch` -> `not_entered`), then `entry_unverifiable`. It never names a wrong game; any timely `confirmed` or `unit_unverified` receipt — earlier, or later on an unresolved M9 re-check — outranks a wrong-game or absence observation (whereas `wrong_round`, `ambiguous`, and `unsupported_duplicate_selection` do not satisfy `entered_game_unverified`). With no `entered`, `entered_game_unverified`, or `entered_late` receipt, it reads `not_entered` when a bound receipt saw `no_pick` or `mismatch`, else `entry_unverifiable`.
