## Blocking findings

1. **A correction can be on disk before the scheduler sends a pick chosen on the old observation.** On Mar 25 at 15:25 ET, the scheduler chose a single on streak 46 and saver used. While delivery awaited the game-status response, the real dated CLI installed streak 1 and saver not earned. The operator received the single labeled “Streak: 1.” The pick kept `chosen_on.streak=46` and the decision recorded a delivered single. The control with the same corrected observation present at selection sent a double. The next-morning check reported `entered` in both runs because the operator followed the DM. `orchestrator.py:284–308` stamps the selection; `scheduler.py:1255–1273` rereads the observation, exempts the current selection from the comparison, and formats the message with the newer streak. Reproduction: `late_correction` and `early_correction_control` in [opening_edges.json](opening_edges.json). **Smallest fix:** compare the stamped dated identity against the final live read even for a fresh selection. Refuse and alert on mismatch, leaving a later check or fallback to choose again.
2. **A lost response can cause two Opening Night pick DMs.** The real DM client submitted Batter 27240 at 16:05. The captured endpoint accepted it, then reading the response raised `TimeoutError`. The scheduler cleared `delivery_attempted` and sent the same body to the same operator at 17:05. The pick and delivered decision represented one send and the pick retained only `wire-2`. The no-retry flag in `dm.py:87` covers the HTTP helper. The blanket handler at `scheduler.py:1295–1316` reopens the day. This handler predates the range and is still reachable in the required live DM path. Reproduction: `transport_retry` in [opening_edges.json](opening_edges.json). **Smallest fix:** clear the attempt marker only for failures established to precede acceptance. Retain it and page the operator to verify delivery when the response is ambiguous.
3. **After a restart, the entry watchdog can instruct the operator to enter obsolete, undelivered picks.** On Mar 25 at 15:25, the scheduler persisted the attempt for a streak-1 double, then died before the transport accepted it. At 15:25:30 the operator corrected to streak 46, saver used. The restart finalized `locked_unconfirmed` and logged the unknown outcome. At 15:40 the watchdog sent “Batter 27250 + DD Batter 27251 … Fix it now!” with 25 minutes left. The recommendation transport saw zero sends. The control at streak 46 chose a single. The accepted unconfirmed lock is preserved by `scheduler.py:3065–3079`. The entry watchdog treats that lock as authority at `cli.py:1934–1939` and issues the instruction at `cli.py:2096–2104`. Reproduction: `crash_then_corrected` in [opening_edges.json](opening_edges.json). **Smallest fix:** keep the duplicate-send lock and replace the named entry instruction on an unconfirmed delivery with a verification alert. Tell the operator to check their DM and MLB before entering anything.
4. **The saver warning points to a CLI path that can disable an otherwise usable automatic observation.** With an automatic streak of 11 and a 2027 saver flag of `not_earned`, the EOD warning said to correct the streak “with bts set-contest-streak” if MLB showed the saver was still unearned. Following that guidance with `--streak 0` exited 0 and installed a legacy override. Status then refused all live use. The explicit dated command recovered it. See `health/contest_state.py:179–188`, `cli.py:1537–1599` and `season_contract.py:118–120`. Reproduction: `saver_instructions` in [opening_edges.json](opening_edges.json). **Smallest fix:** put the complete dated template, with the configured picks directory and all three values read from MLB, in that warning.
