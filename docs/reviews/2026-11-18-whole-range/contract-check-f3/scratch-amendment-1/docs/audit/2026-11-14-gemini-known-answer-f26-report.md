- **File and line:** [f26_studio.py](file:///Users/eric/projects/bts-agy-ka/scripts/audit/c2_framing/f26_studio.py#L221) (`seed_rank1` trusts `man["calendar"]`) and [f26_studio.py](file:///Users/eric/projects/bts-agy-ka/scripts/audit/c2_framing/f26_studio.py#L235-L239) (`aggregate` checks only `s["calendar"] != seeds[0]["calendar"]` and never calls `calendar_2026(inputs, pins)`).
- **Required change:** Derive `expected = calendar_2026(inputs, pins)` inside `aggregate`, verify `expected` is nonempty and equals every seed's `man["calendar"]` and `sha256(F.canonical(expected)) == res["calendar_sha256"]`, and reject mismatches with `F.RunInvalid` (violates [prereg §6](file:///Users/eric/projects/bts-agy-ka/docs/sota_audit/2026-10-09-prereg-c2-framing-2026-test.md#L91): *"A shared omission is incomplete too"*).
