### B1. Crash + same-day `reconcile_results` permanently loses a scored day via `applied_dates` (`327beae`, `4199360`)
- **Where:** `src/bts/picks.py:559-567`, `608-611`, `661-662`, `1194`, `1317-1320`
- **Input / state:** Day `D` (`2027-05-02`) resolves (`hit`, or saver-absorbing `miss` at `streak=10`). `update_streak(..., picks_dir, date=D)` writes `streak.json` with `applied_dates=[..., D]`, and the process crashes before `save_pick` writes `result` in `D/pick.json`. Before `01:00` `bts check-results --date D` runs, same-day `reconcile_results` (`picks.py:1194`) runs `_replay_season_streak(picks_dir, year, D)` (`picks.py:1317-1320`), skips unresolved `D` (`picks.py:661-662`), and calls `save_streak` (`picks.py:559-567`).
- **Wrong production outcome:** `save_streak` rolls `streak`/`saver_available` back to `D-1` while keeping `D` in `applied_dates`. When `01:00` `bts check-results --date D` resolves `D/pick.json` and calls `update_streak(..., date=D)`, `picks.py:610-611` sees `D in applied_dates` and returns unchanged. `D` is marked `'hit'` (or `'miss'`) on disk while `streak` remains permanently 1 low (`1` vs `2`, stranded at `2` vs `3` across a subsequent `'void'`) or `saver_available` stays permanently `True` after consuming the saver.
- **Verification:** **Verified by running** `.codex-review/agy-ka2/scratch/probe_streak_reconcile.py` (`PROBE 1A`, `1B`, `1D`) and reading `src/bts/picks.py`.
- **Smallest fix:** When `reconcile_results` persists `_replay_season_streak`, reset/sync `applied_dates` to only the resolved dates included in that replay (do not retain unresolved `D`).

### B4. `DATA_BUILD_SEASON=$(date +\%Y)` breaks pre-Opening-Day `bts preview` and `bts data sync-to-r2` (`1137cbe`)
- **Where:** `scripts/cron-setup-hetzner.sh:53, 96`; `src/bts/data/build.py:326, 349, 393`
