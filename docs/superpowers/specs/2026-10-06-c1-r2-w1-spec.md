# W1 spec: W-deliver, W-postponed, W-decision, W-mode (DRAFT, for spec review before code)

**What this is:** the second piece of the C1 rank-2 watchdog (build plan W1). It implements registration rows W-deliver, W-postponed, W-decision and W-mode, plus the schedule rules in §3, from `docs/sota_audit/2026-10-04-prereg-c1-watchdog.md` (FROZEN).
- **Evidence:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-c1-r2-w1-evidence-map.md` (the "map").
- **The skeleton** is W0 (`src/bts/watchdog/`).
- **Production code:** review until SIGN, then D7.

## 0. Common rules
- **Job:** `bts watchdog run fast` every 5 minutes, every day, all ET hours. The cron line arrives with the intent-aware install, under D7. The job runs these four checks; later pieces add W-entry and restart status.
- **Target dates:**
  - each run checks **today** (ET) and **carries** every earlier date within a 3-day horizon whose incident is still open (an open episode in the notification state) or whose day disposition is not yet final;
  - each result names its own `et_date`.
- **Read-only (R1):**
  - **Picks:** read raw bytes of `data/picks/<D>.json`, `<D>/decision.json`, `<D>/scheduler_state.json` and the archives `<D>/{stale_pick,deferred_fallback,refused_delivery}_*.json`, via `Path.read_bytes`. Never `load_state` (it renames corrupt files).
  - **Config:** read with `tomllib` from `~/.bts-orchestrator.toml` (`--config`).
  - **Heartbeat:** `data/.heartbeat`.
  - **Crontab:** `crontab -l`, a read-only subprocess with a 10 s timeout and `stdin=DEVNULL`.
- **Network (R6):** at most **one** unauthenticated MLB request per target date per run: `GET https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/schedule?sportId=1&date=<D>`, with a 10 s timeout and no retry loop.
  - **On failure,** every predicate that needs refreshed times or status is `unverifiable` ("schedule unavailable"). The run still completes.
  - No authenticated request, no cookie read.
- **Times:** ET-aware clocks via the W0 seam. Pick `game_time` is UTC, and a naive value is read as UTC.
  - **Refreshed first pitch** is the schedule `gameDate` for the game, falling back to the pick's `game_time` only when the game is absent from the schedule; that absence is itself evidence for W-postponed.
  - **The cutoff** is min over the selected legs of (refreshed first pitch) − `SUBMISSION_CUTOFF_MIN`.
  - **Comparison:** at or after the cutoff is "past"; equality is past, matching the scheduler guard.
- **Mode gating (R4):** W-deliver's delivery expectations apply only under `enter`. Under `research`, W-deliver reports `verified` with "research mode: no delivery expected", and W-mode handles breaches. An intent problem makes the intent-dependent predicates `unverifiable` (W-mode reports the configuration alert).
- **Grace windows:**
  - **Decision-after-delivery (5 min):** the decision is written after `_trigger_live_forward_capture_on_lock` (timeout 10 s) and after the send. A delivered pick without a decision is `pending` for 5 min after `delivered_at`, then `fault`.
  - **Poll-tolerance (none):** detection is at the next poll; no extra grace beyond the cutoff.
- **Statuses:** each check returns one result per (check, date, slot-or-day), using W0 statuses. `selection` is `<batter_id>@<game_pk>` (`+` joins two legs), or `day` for day-level results.

## 1. W-deliver
**Under `enter`, for target date D:**

| Evidence | Result |
|---|---|
| The schedule lists no regular-season games for D | `verified`: no games, no delivery expected |
| decision `delivery_status == delivered` and `pick_was_delivered(pick)` | compare `delivered_at` with the refreshed cutoff. Strictly before gives `verified`; at or after gives `fault` (incident `I-late-delivery`, "a confirmed receipt at/after cutoff"). A missing or unparseable `delivered_at` gives `unverifiable` |
| decision `delivery_status == locked_unconfirmed` and the pick is not delivered | `fault`: "scoreable locked_unconfirmed commit without a receipt" |
| decision `action == skip`, `source == mdp` | `verified`: MDP skip disposition |
| no decision, `scheduler_state.final_skip_candidate` set, `committed_pick_written` false | `verified`: skip pending its end-of-day record; W-decision follows the record |
| no decision; a live **undelivered** candidate pick (no skip evidence) | before its refreshed cutoff: `pending`, plus the risk rule below. At or after it: `fault` "persistent overdue undelivered candidate" (incident `I-overdue-candidate`) |
| a `refused_delivery_*` archive for D | `fault`: "delivery refused past cutoff" (from the archive's `cutoff_et` / `late_min`). Once a later pick is delivered before **its** cutoff, the day disposition is that delivery, and the refusal stays a distinct fault (I-201 class) |
| a `stale_pick_*` archive (`committed_game_past_cutoff`) and no delivered pick on an **earlier** game | `fault`: "missed early game", unless an MDP-skip disposition exists. A later chosen game that was delivered is reported separately and is not a fault |
| a `deferred_fallback_*` archive | not a fault by itself (a policy deferral). The day is judged by its final disposition |
| no pick file, no decision, no archives, no skip evidence, and the refreshed schedule's **last** game of D is past its cutoff | `fault`: "no day disposition" (incident `I-no-disposition`). Before that it is `pending` |

**Pre-cutoff risk:**
- **When:** a live undelivered candidate exists, the refreshed cutoff is in the future, and the scheduler heartbeat says `SLEEPING` with `sleeping_until` at or after that cutoff.
- **What:** that is a `fault` "no scheduled wake precedes the candidate cutoff" (incident `I-wake-after-cutoff`).
- **Only evidence used:** the persisted current wake. Planned check times are not persisted (map gap 1), so no other wake is claimed.

**Text:** post-cutoff faults never say "enter now".

## 2. W-postponed
**Slots:** for the candidate pick (live) and every delivered or committed slot of D (both double-down legs), using the refreshed schedule.

| Schedule evidence for the slot's `game_pk` | Result |
|---|---|
| absent from D's schedule | `fault` "game missing from schedule" |
| `detailedState` postponed / cancelled / canceled | `fault` "postponed/cancelled game" for a **delivered or committed** slot (I-203 class); for an undelivered candidate, `fault` with severity text "candidate on a postponed game" |
| `officialDate` earlier than D (a resumed suspended game) | `fault` "resume-day eligibility" |
| otherwise | `verified` |

**Wording:**
- Every result says "current status at <observation time>".
- It never claims the status at send time: that is not persisted (map gap 3).
- When the slate `bts_slate_v2` row for the slot carries a `status`, it is reported as supplementary evidence only.

**Out of scope:** suspension after play, and its grade, belong to W-grade.

## 3. W-decision
**For each target date D:**

| Evidence | Result |
|---|---|
| a delivered pick (or `delivery_attempted` true) with **no** scoreable decision | `pending` until `delivered_at + 5 min` (or `pick_locked_at + 5 min`), then `fault` "receipt/attempted send without a matching decision" |
| decision `scoreable` true and `action == skip` | `fault` "score-bearing skip" |
| a pick with a non-null `result`, while the decision is absent or non-scoreable **and** the pick is not delivered | `fault` "score-bearing preview" |
| a scoreable decision whose `primary.batter_id` / `game_pk` differ from the pick file's `pick` | `fault` "decision selection inconsistent with the pick" |
| a `double` decision whose `double_down` differs from the pick's `double_down` | `fault` (same). A `single` record's `double_down` is the runner-up and is **not** compared (map §2 caveat) |
| decision `primary` null on a commit | `unverifiable` ("commit record without a selection"); the pick file is the selection |
| `private_locked` | consistent with `scheduler_state.pick_locked` (private locking evidence can live in state) |
| a past date (before today) with a candidate pick or archives but no decision and no skip evidence, by 06:15 ET the next day | `fault` "unresolved day disposition" |
| a no-games day | `verified` "no games, no disposition expected" |

**Unreadable or invalid input:** an unreadable or invalid `decision.json` (a parse failure or a schema mismatch) is `unverifiable`, never "absent".

## 4. W-mode
**Inputs:**
- **Intent:** `check_entry_intent(load_config(--config))` (P4).
- **Installed crontab:** `crontab -l` lines with `# BTS-HETZNER`. The entry checker counts as installed only if an **uncommented** line contains `bts check-pick-entered`.
- **Recommendation delivery evidence:** today's and yesterday's decision `delivery_status`, and the pick channel fields.

**Results:**

| Evidence | Result |
|---|---|
| an intent problem (missing, invalid, or disagreeing with the effective delivery mode) | `fault` "configuration: <problem>" (incident `I-config-intent`) |
| `enter` and no installed entry cron | `fault` "enter without an active entry checker" |
| `research` and an installed entry cron | `fault` "research with an active entry checker" (the 10/06 hazard) |
| `research` and a decision `delivered`, or a pick with channel fields on a date at or after the intent was set | `fault` "recommendation delivered in research mode" |
| `enter` and a `private_locked` decision | `fault` "private commit under enter" |
| `crontab -l` fails (other than "no crontab for") | `unverifiable` |
| otherwise | `verified` |

**Never:** it changes no configuration and no crontab (R1). Operational watchdog DMs are not recommendation delivery.

## 5. Tests (gate 1 shaped)
**Fixtures:** each predicate has red-then-green fixtures driving the **real** `bts watchdog run fast` CLI, with the schedule HTTP, `crontab -l`, the clock and the DM transport patched at their leaves, never the predicate.

**Cases:**
- **Cutoff edges:** equality at the cutoff, an earlier double-down leg, a refreshed game time different from the pick's `game_time`, UTC/ET date disagreement, midnight carry-over, DST days, early and late games.
- **Days:** a no-games day; schedule failure (`unverifiable`).
- **W-deliver:** each row of §1.
- **W-postponed:** a postponed delivered slot, a postponed candidate, a missing game, a resumed game.
- **W-decision:**
  - the transient decision window (pending, then fault after 5 min);
  - a score-bearing skip and a score-bearing preview;
  - selection mismatch: a single with a runner-up `double_down` is not a mismatch; a real double mismatch is;
  - an unresolved past day; an invalid decision.
- **W-mode:** missing cron under `enter`, active cron under `research`, a commented-out cron line, crontab failure, an intent problem, delivery in research.

**Confinement:** gate 2 (W0's sandbox gate) runs the `fast` job with fixtures, plus the `crontab -l` subprocess.

## 6. Open questions for the spec review
1. Is the 5-minute decision-after-delivery grace sound, given the 10 s capture subprocess plus send latency?
2. Is the heartbeat `sleeping_until` the right and only basis for the pre-cutoff risk rule, given that planned times are not persisted?
3. W-postponed "candidate on a postponed game": fault, or `pending`, since the scheduler normally regenerates it?
4. The carry horizon (3 days) and the "unresolved day disposition" deadline (06:15 next day).
5. Reading `crontab -l` from the watchdog: acceptable under R1/R6 as a read-only local subprocess?
